# 🚘 Driver & Spot Owner (Parker) Platform Rules & Business Policies

This document establishes the platform business rules, reservation guidelines, cancellation policies, and operational protocols for **Drivers** and **Parking Spot Owners (Parkers)**.

---

## 🚗 Section 1: Driver Platform Rules

### 1. Booking & Reservation Policy
- **Minimum Booking Window**: Reservations must be booked at least 15 minutes prior to the start time.
- **Minimum Duration**: Minimum parking duration is 1 hour. Charges are rounded up to the nearest full hour.
- **Accurate Vehicle Information**: The driver must provide an accurate vehicle registration number and vehicle model during booking. Spot owners reserve the right to report unauthorized vehicles.

### 2. Check-In & Parking Protocol
- **Arrival Window**: Drivers are granted a 15-minute grace period after their scheduled start time.
- **Designated Bay Compliance**: Drivers must park strictly within the assigned spot bounds and adhere to any facility-specific rules (e.g., EV charging etiquette, clearance heights).
- **QR Code Verification**: Upon arrival, the driver may present the in-app QR code for owner/valet verification if required.

### 3. Overstay & Extension Policy
- **Extensions**: Drivers can extend active bookings through the app provided the spot does not have an immediate subsequent confirmed reservation.
- **Unapproved Overstay**: If a driver fails to vacate or extend past the grace period, an overstay penalty fee (1.5x hourly rate) may be assessed automatically.

### 4. Cancellation & Refund Policy
- **Free Cancellation**: Drivers may cancel for a full refund up to 1 hour before the scheduled start time.
- **Late Cancellation**: Cancellations made less than 1 hour prior to start time incur a 50% fee.
- **No-Show**: If the driver fails to check in without cancelling, no refund is issued.

---

## 🅿️ Section 2: Spot Owner (Parker) Platform Rules

### 1. Spot Listing & Accuracy Guidelines
- **Accurate Description**: Spot owners must provide accurate details regarding spot type (*Covered, Open, Underground, Valet*), clearance height, access instructions, and available amenities (*CCTV, EV charger, security guard*).
- **Physical Availability**: Listed spots must be vacant, unobstructed, and safely accessible during all listed active hours.

### 2. Request Handling & Responsiveness
- **Booking Requests**: Owners must accept or decline pending driver reservation requests within 30 minutes. Unanswered requests are automatically expired to protect driver experience.
- **Instant Booking**: If instant booking is enabled, reservations are auto-confirmed upon successful payment authorization.

### 3. Owner Cancellation & Penalties
- **Host Cancellations**: Spot owners cannot cancel confirmed bookings except in verified emergency situations (e.g., physical obstruction, property damage).
- Repeated host-initiated cancellations will lower owner visibility ranking or result in account suspension.

### 4. Payouts & Revenue Policy
- **Payout Frequency**: Owner earnings from completed bookings are compiled and processed on a scheduled basis (weekly or monthly).
- **Platform Fee**: The platform retains a standard 10% operational commission on each completed reservation.
- **Dispute Resolution**: If a dispute arises between a driver and owner (e.g., inaccessible bay, vehicle damage), the platform support team reviews timestamps, QR logs, and communication logs to resolve claims.
